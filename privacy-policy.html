Privacy Policy

Galaxship Space Dodge · Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Galaxship Space Dodge (the "Game"), developed by Rehan Ali and Talal Haider ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it on Android.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, no login and no sign-up.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location ourselves.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings locally on your device, for example: current level, coins, high score, unlocked and equipped ships and planets, boss attempts, tutorial status, and sound, music and vibration settings. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewards earned) and diagnostic data

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your choices and the law in your region. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Your consent and choices

  • Consent: In regions where it is required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy options" button in the Game's main menu, when it is shown.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded ads are optional: You choose when to watch a rewarded ad (for example to revive or unlock items).

5. In-app purchases

The Game does not offer in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing or by watching optional rewarded ads.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help address it.

7. Data security and retention

Because your game data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

8. Deleting your data

To delete all game data, uninstall the Game or clear its app data in your device settings. To manage advertising data, use the choices described in section 4.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
